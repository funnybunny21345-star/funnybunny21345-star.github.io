🐶 Puppy Shorts

A personal automation tool for uploading eligible puppy clips from Google Drive to a connected YouTube channel.

What it does

Puppy Shorts selects videos from folders that the owner configures in Google Drive, checks basic video format requirements, and uploads authorized puppy clips to YouTube Shorts. It can choose puppy-themed titles and owner-selected hashtags.

How it works

The owner signs in with their Google account to authorize access to Google Drive and uploads to YouTube. A scheduled uploader can attempt a new upload every eight hours. Clips are moved between Ready, Processing, Posted, and other queue folders to keep track of their status.

Who is it for?

This is a privately operated personal tool, not a public service. It only uploads videos the owner owns or is authorized to reuse.
