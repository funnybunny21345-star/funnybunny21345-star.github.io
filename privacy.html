Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Puppy Shorts is a personally operated utility to upload authorized puppy videos from the operator's Google Drive folders to the operator's YouTube channel. It is not currently offered as a public app.

Google account permissions

The uploader requests Google authorization for Google Drive file access and YouTube video uploads. The granted Drive permission can technically provide access beyond the configured folders; the uploader itself is configured to list, download, and move video files in specified queue folders. It also uploads videos and metadata, including titles, descriptions, and hashtags, to the chosen YouTube channel.

Information used

The uploader processes the names, IDs, file contents, descriptions, and processing status of videos in configured Drive folders. It uses Google authorization credentials to perform the configured actions. The static website itself does not ask visitors to sign in with Google or submit their video files.

Storage and sharing

Video files remain in Google Drive and are temporarily downloaded by the uploader for inspection and uploading. Google processes videos and metadata sent to YouTube. Authorization credentials are stored locally in a private token file or, if deployed to the cloud, in a configured secrets store. The uploader does not sell Google user data. If the operator enables Discord failure alerts, failure messages such as video filenames and errors may be sent to Discord. This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web-request data under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

The operator controls the videos in Drive and YouTube and may remove them using those services. Temporary video downloads are intended to be discarded at the end of a processing run. The operator can remove stored authorization tokens from their own computer or cloud setup and can revoke Google access at Google Account connections.
